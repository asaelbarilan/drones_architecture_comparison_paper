Method and limits

All nine PNG request payloads match the frozen sources byte for byte. Original prompts, schemas and both raw response channels are archived. The configured JSON channel is thinking. Truth annotations and numerical ranges were not sent to the model. 801 replayed poses and six reconstructed source images match the C2 recording.

Correction to the earlier interpretation: the request at 26 s already returned distance=5 while its source image was red-filled. That label does not establish that the target had left the camera.

The localization-only task differs from the combined waypoint task. These selected frames do not establish flight reliability or a causal architectural advantage.
